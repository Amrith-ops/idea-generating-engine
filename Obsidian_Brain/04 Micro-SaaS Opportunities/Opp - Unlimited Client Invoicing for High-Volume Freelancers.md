## 🎯 1. Target ICP & Market Opportunity
- **Ideal Customer Profile**: **High-Volume Freelance Consultants and Small Agencies** (Marketing & Creative Services)
- **Company Size**: `small_business` (1–50 employees)
- **Buying Process**: 100% Self-Serve credit card checkout.

## 💥 2. Incumbent Flaw & Why This Wins
**Attacked Products**:
- [[FreshBooks]]

**Value Proposition**:
A streamlined invoicing tool that removes the 'success tax' by offering unlimited clients and seats for a flat, predictable fee. It focuses exclusively on getting paid and tracking time without the enterprise accounting bloat.

## 🛠️ 3. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Core Features
- [ ] **Unlimited client profiles and unlimited active invoices**
- [ ] **Flat-rate pricing model (no per-seat or per-client increases)**
- [ ] **Automated recurring billing with Stripe and PayPal integration**
- [ ] **Clean, 1-click expense tracking via mobile photo upload**
- [ ] **White-labeled client portal for professional invoice approval**

## 💰 4. Pricing & Monetization Model
- **Strategy**: $25/month flat rate for unlimited users/clients
- **Target Price**: $29 – $79 / month flat rate (zero per-seat fees).

## 📣 5. Launch & Distribution Channels
- **Primary Channels**: ProductHunt, Indie Hackers, and targeted ads for 'FreshBooks Alternatives'
- **Landing Page Hook**: *"The dead-simple, zero-bloat opp - unlimited client invoicing for high-volume freelancers built specifically for High-Volume Freelance Consultants and Small Agencies."*
