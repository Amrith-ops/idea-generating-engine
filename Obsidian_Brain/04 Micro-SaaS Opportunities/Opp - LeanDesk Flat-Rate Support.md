## 🎯 1. Target ICP & Market Opportunity
- **Ideal Customer Profile**: **Bootstrapped SaaS Founders** (B2B SaaS)
- **Company Size**: `small_business` (1–50 employees)
- **Buying Process**: 100% Self-Serve credit card checkout.

## 💥 2. Incumbent Flaw & Why This Wins
**Attacked Products**:
- [[Zendesk]]
- [[Salesforce Service Cloud]]
- [[Zoho Desk]]

**Value Proposition**:
A no-nonsense help desk that replaces per-seat pricing with a single flat monthly fee. Includes high-tier features like a public Knowledge Base and simple automations out of the box, specifically for teams of 2-15 people.

## 🛠️ 3. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Core Features
- [ ] **Shared Email Inbox with Collision Detection**
- [ ] **Public & Private Markdown Knowledge Base**
- [ ] **Flat-Rate Pricing (Unlimited Agents up to 20)**
- [ ] **One-Click Data Import from Zendesk/Freshdesk**
- [ ] **Basic Slack Integration for Ticket Notifications**

## 💰 4. Pricing & Monetization Model
- **Strategy**: $49/month flat rate
- **Target Price**: $29 – $79 / month flat rate (zero per-seat fees).

- **Primary Channels**: Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, B2B SaaS Facebook Groups
