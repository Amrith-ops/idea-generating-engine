## 🔍 G2 Pain Point Triage by Company Tier

### 🟢 Small Business (1–50 Employees) — *Micro-SaaS Goldmine*
- **Founder** (Technology):
  > *"The onboarding experience was incredibly difficult for our lean team. The platform is built with so many enterprise-level knobs and levers that it took us weeks just to get a basic workflow running, lacking the simplicity a small startup needs."*

### 🟡 Mid-Market (51–500 Employees) — *Satellite Bridge Opportunity*
- **Operations Manager** (E-Commerce):
  > *"The per-seat pricing model is becoming a major burden. As we expand our support department to handle seasonal spikes, the escalating costs for every additional agent license significantly strain our budget compared to flat-fee alternatives."*

### 🔴 Enterprise (1,000+ Employees) — *Anti-Pattern Reference (What to Cut)*
- *Enterprise Complaint*: "The interface has become noticeably sluggish.
