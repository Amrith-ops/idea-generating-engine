## 🔍 G2 Pain Point Triage by Company Tier

### 🟢 Small Business (1–50 Employees) — *Micro-SaaS Goldmine*
- **CFO** (E-Commerce):
  > *"The entry cost is incredibly high for a growing business, with Year 1 implementation and licensing typically running between $100,000 and $300,000. Additionally, the 'migration friction' is real; the system is designed in a way that makes it genuinely difficult to offboard your data once you have committed to the ecosystem."*

### 🟡 Mid-Market (51–500 Employees) — *Satellite Bridge Opportunity*
- **Accounts Payable Lead** (Retail):
  > *"The platform lacks the native capability to automate expense allocations based on specific percentages per vendor. This missing feature forces our team into manual spreadsheet calculations and line-item entries for every invoice, negating some of the automation benefits we expected from an ERP."*

### 🔴 Enterprise (1,000+ Employees) — *Anti-Pattern Reference (What to Cut)*
- *Enterprise Complaint*: "While it is marketed as a solution for those drowning in five or more different softwares, the migration process is an uphill battle. The friction involved in the transition is so high that it becomes a 'sticky' product simply because leaving is too painful, rather than the user experience being seamless." *(Note: Strip this complex requirement when building a simple SMB competitor)*
