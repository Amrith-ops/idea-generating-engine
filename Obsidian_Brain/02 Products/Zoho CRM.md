## 🔍 G2 Pain Point Triage by Company Tier

### 🟢 Small Business (1–50 Employees) — *Micro-SaaS Goldmine*
- **Startup Founder** (B2B SaaS):
  > *"The learning curve is significantly steeper than advertised. The interface feels cluttered and unintuitive, which makes onboarding new team members difficult without extensive training. For a small, bootstrapped team that needs to move fast, the 'overwhelming' customization options actually become a hindrance compared to more opinionated, straightforward CRMs."*
- **Marketing Lead** (Real Estate):
  > *"The user interface is a major bottleneck; it feels dated and cluttered, which leads to a slow workflow for my agents. Navigating between Zoho Campaigns and the CRM isn't as seamless as promised without heavy manual configuration. It feels like a 'wide but shallow' platform that attempts to do everything but lacks the UI polish needed for daily efficiency."*

### 🟡 Mid-Market (51–500 Employees) — *Satellite Bridge Opportunity*
- **Sales Operations Manager** (E-Commerce):
  > *"While the entry-level pricing is attractive, the value proposition diminishes as you grow. We found that essential advanced features and specific analytics required for our scale were only available on the Enterprise and Ultimate plans. This 'pricing trap' makes the software feel much more expensive than the initial marketing suggests once you need professional-grade tools."*
