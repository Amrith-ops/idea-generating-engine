## 🔍 G2 Pain Point Triage by Company Tier

### 🟢 Small Business (1–50 Employees) — *Micro-SaaS Goldmine*
- **Founder** (E-Commerce):
  > *"While the integration with Outlook and Teams is a strong point, the opaque pricing model and implementation costs make it a challenging solution. The interface is quite unintuitive for a small team, making it hard to justify the total cost of ownership compared to more modern, streamlined CRMs."*

### 🟡 Mid-Market (51–500 Employees) — *Satellite Bridge Opportunity*
- **Operations Manager** (B2B SaaS):
  > *"The learning curve for new users is incredibly steep because the system is packed with too many features that aren't immediately relevant. Setting up and configuring the platform to match our specific business logic was overwhelming and required far more technical overhead than we anticipated."*

### 🔴 Enterprise (1,000+ Employees) — *Anti-Pattern Reference (What to Cut)*
- *Enterprise Complaint*: "The platform can feel very clunky and slow during day-to-day operations. Navigating between sales management features and customer invoices involves too many steps. The combination of an unintuitive interface and the sheer complexity of the configuration makes it difficult for our agents to maintain high productivity." *(Note: Strip this complex requirement when building a simple SMB competitor)*
