## 🔍 G2 Pain Point Triage by Company Tier

### 🟢 Small Business (1–50 Employees) — *Micro-SaaS Goldmine*
- **SaaS Founder** (B2B SaaS):
  > *"The Knowledge Base feature is gated behind a paid plan, which is a major hurdle for startups trying to reduce ticket volume through self-service guides. We also found the pricing tiers to be rigid; we wanted the entry-level plan with just a few specific add-ons, but the platform forces you into the most expensive tier to access minor yet essential capabilities."*

### 🟡 Mid-Market (51–500 Employees) — *Satellite Bridge Opportunity*
- **Support Operations Manager** (Technology):
  > *"Configuring advanced workflows and custom modules comes with an incredibly steep learning curve. The interface feels cluttered and busy, with many functionalities feeling scattered across the platform, making it difficult for the team to master the software during a standard trial period."*
- **Sales Operations Lead** (E-Commerce):
  > *"We've encountered significant performance lag when the ticket volume increases or when working with complex configurations. Additionally, the platform is missing practical extensions that our team relies on, such as reply-due timers and deal-sequencing, which are vital for maintaining our response standards."*
