## 🔍 G2 Pain Point Triage by Company Tier

### 🟢 Small Business (1–50 Employees) — *Micro-SaaS Goldmine*
- **Freelance Consultant** (Marketing & Advertising):
  > *"The pricing structure is misleading for growing businesses. The 'Lite' plan limits you to only 5 billable clients, which is practically impossible for any active freelancer. Moving to 'Plus' for 50 clients is a massive jump in cost that doesn't feel justified by the incremental features provided, effectively trapping users into higher tiers as soon as they find any success."*
- **Agency Founder** (Creative Services):
  > *"The platform has become increasingly bloated over the last few years. What used to be a simple, streamlined invoicing tool is now cluttered with project management and advanced accounting features that are difficult to navigate. Reconciling bank accounts has become a multi-step chore compared to the original intuitive interface."*
