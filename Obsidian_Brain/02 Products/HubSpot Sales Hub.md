## 🔍 G2 Pain Point Triage by Company Tier

### 🟢 Small Business (1–50 Employees) — *Micro-SaaS Goldmine*
- **Founder** (B2B SaaS):
  > *"The pricing model is increasingly frustrating for early-stage companies. While they claim you can start for free, the transition to 'Core Seats' for Sales Hub leads to a pricing trap where costs escalate rapidly due to add-ons, contact overages, and seat minimums that make it one of the most expensive CRMs on the market."*

### 🟡 Mid-Market (51–500 Employees) — *Satellite Bridge Opportunity*
- **SDR Manager** (Marketing Services):
  > *"HubSpot Sales Hub is a mediocre SDR execution tool because many critical prospecting features are locked behind high-tier paywalls. Specifically, sequences are limited to email-only, which hinders multi-channel outreach strategies, and there are occasional glitches and delays that slow down high-volume outbound workflows."*
- **Operations Manager** (Healthcare):
  > *"The platform has a very steep learning curve for advanced features, and we have experienced significant delays and technical issues that take a long time to resolve. For a company in a regulated space like healthcare, the implementation fees and the hidden costs of one-time onboarding make it feel less efficient than advertised."*
